---
name: scr-references
description: "Trigger: adding, checking or citing references in a Scribe manuscript or references.bib. Maintain traceable bibliographic references without fabricated evidence."
---

Use supplied bibliographic metadata or a configured authorized search tool. Record what was
verified and what remains pending. Metadata verification does not establish claim support.
When a search integration is unavailable, retain an explicit unverified marker. Do not invent
authors, DOIs or publication details. The manuscript language does not change source titles.
